Two ways to explore.

GBU Campus is a sample project and assignment for the GBU course. Its daytime tour and fictional haunted-night game give students a working Three.js project to explore, adapt and learn from.

Students use this sample to practise GitHub operations, hosting and publishing, and AI-assisted programming for 3D gaming. The campus layout is interpreted and scaled for exploration; this is not an official university tour. The night mode contains fictional horror imagery.

Walk through the day tour, explore its landmarks, or try the night game's challenges. The experience runs in your browser; no account is needed.
